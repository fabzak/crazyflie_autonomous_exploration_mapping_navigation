REFERENCE DESIGN DEFECT - DL-001
================================
Title:    Detector armed for one sample under stale mission intent
Found by: C port safety-invariant I8 (lifecycle_selftest.c:359-361)
Present in: BOTH oracle_datum_lifecycle.py (frozen reference) and datum_lifecycle.c
Status:   NOT FIXED. Oracle deliberately left unmodified per task rule.

DEFECT
  The mission-phase freshness gate is evaluated INSIDE the DATUM_VALID handler.
  The edges that ENTER DATUM_VALID - DATUM_ACQUIRE (stability satisfied),
  DATUM_REVALIDATE (dwell satisfied) and RECOVER - do not re-check phase
  freshness at the instant of entry. The gate therefore takes effect one
  sample AFTER entry, not on it.

EXPOSURE
  Exactly one control sample (25 ms at 40 Hz) during which the module
  publishes detector_enabled=true AND xy_allowed=true while mission intent
  is stale (measured at phase_age = 1.0 s, i.e. 2x DL_PHASE_TIMEOUT_MS).
  This is fail-OPEN behaviour in a contract whose premise is fail-closed.

REPRODUCER  (repro_defect_i8.py / repro_defect_i8.c - identical output)
  One fresh HOLD_LAYER sample, then 24 consecutive stale samples:
    sample 17  age 1.0s  DATUM_ACQUIRE  detector=False  xy_allowed=False
    sample 18  age 1.0s  DATUM_ACQUIRE  detector=False  xy_allowed=False
    sample 19  age 1.0s  DATUM_VALID    detector=True   xy_allowed=True   <-- DEFECT
    sample 20  age 1.0s  SETTLING       detector=False  xy_allowed=False

CANDIDATE CORRECTION (design review, NOT applied)
  Make phase freshness a precondition of the ENTRY edge into DATUM_VALID,
  not a check performed by the DATUM_VALID handler; equivalently, evaluate
  the gate once per sample before state dispatch. Must be re-proven in
  simulation, then re-ported and re-differentiated.
